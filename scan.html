Scan to play Slapshot Arena

Point a phone camera at the code. The game opens straight in the browser, no app store, no install.

jtpetrotech.github.io/slapshot-arena
  1. Scan the code — the phone camera app is enough, no QR reader needed.
  2. Tap the link, turn the phone sideways. The rink wants the width.
  3. Keep it on the home screen. iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the browser menu, then Add to Home screen.

Open to anyone — no account, no sign-in, nothing to install. Once it has loaded, the game keeps working with the phone offline.
